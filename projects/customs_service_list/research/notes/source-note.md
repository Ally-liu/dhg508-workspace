# Source note: the 1939 Customs *Service List*

## What the document is

China, The Maritime Customs. IV. Service Series: No. 1. *Service List*,
Sixty-fifth Issue (corrected to 1st June 1939). Shanghai: Statistical
Department of the Inspectorate General of Customs, 1939.

An official serial staff register (題名錄 / 職員錄) printed by the Customs
service itself for internal use. A primary source, and an example of an
institution recording its own personnel.

## Where the copy came from

Internet Archive, item `CustomsServiceList1939`, Public Domain Mark 1.0.
148 leaves, 267 ppi, OCR by ABBYY FineReader 9.0. Used here: printed
pages 1–5 (senior posts only, 103 rows).

Other holdings of the same serial:

- HathiTrust catalogue record 000497281 (*Service list / China, Imperial
  Maritime Customs*).
- Academia Sinica, Institute of Modern History, "Databases of Chinese Maritime
  Customs" — Service List 1875–1947 (mhdb.mh.sinica.edu.tw/customs/web).
- SOAS and the Second Historical Archives of China, Nanjing (CMCS archives).

## Limits of the source

- It is the institution's own record: it shows the categories, the omissions,
  and the distinctions (province for Chinese staff, nationality for foreigners)
  that the service chose to make.
- It is a snapshot: "corrected to 1st June 1939"; anything before or after needs
  another issue.
- Fields are limited: no salary, age, education, or career after 1939.
