# Week 2 — A historical question, two sources, and OCR

## Question

When was the Jingshi Library (京师图书馆), the ancestor of today's National
Library of China, founded — and when did it actually open?

## Answer (provisional, to be checked against sources)

It was approved in **1909**, when the Qing Board of Education (学部) memorialized
the throne to establish a metropolitan library, and it **opened to the public in
1912**, after the Republic replaced the Qing. The gap between "approved" (1909)
and "open" (1912) is what makes the question worth asking: the dynasty that
ordered the library did not live to see it work.
